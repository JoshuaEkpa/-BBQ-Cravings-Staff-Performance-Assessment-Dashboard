📊 Project Overview

The BBQ Cravings Staff Performance Assessment System is an Excel-based performance management and reporting solution designed to automate staff assessments and provide management with a clear view of employee performance.

The system transforms daily staff assessments into weekly and monthly performance results, percentages, ratings, and an interactive management dashboard.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to create a structured and automated staff performance assessment system that would:

- Track staff performance on a daily basis
- Evaluate employees across multiple performance criteria
- Calculate weekly performance automatically
- Calculate monthly average performance
- Convert scores into percentages
- Assign performance ratings
- Provide management with a clear dashboard for decision-making

## ⚙️ System Architecture

The workbook consists of three main components:

### 1. Automation
The Automation sheet serves as the calculation engine.

It contains:

- Staff dropdown
- Month dropdown
- Year dropdown
- Daily assessment scores from Day 1–30
- Weekly calculations
- Monthly calculations
- Percentage calculations
- Performance ratings

### 2. Assessment
The Assessment sheet presents the calculated assessment results in a structured format.

It displays:

- Staff Name
- Performance Criteria
- Week 1
- Week 2
- Week 3
- Week 4
- Week 5
- Monthly Average
- Percentage
- Rating

### 3. Dashboard
The Dashboard provides a management-friendly summary of staff performance.

It displays:

- Date
- Staff
- Criteria
- Week 1–5 performance
- Monthly Average
- Percentage
- Rating

## 📋 Performance Criteria

Staff performance is evaluated using criteria such as:

- Initiative and Positivity
- Communication
- Food Presentation
- Uniforms/Shoe
- General Hygiene/Nails
- Menu Knowledge
- Preparation and Order Accuracy
- Attitude
- Team Work
- Attendance & Lateness
- Work Space Management
- Record Keeping

## 📈 Rating System

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 5 | Excellent |
| 4 | Very Good |
| 3 | Good |
| 2 | Fair |
| 1 | Poor |

## 🔄 Workflow

Staff + Month + Year
        ↓
Daily Scores (1–30)
        ↓
Weekly Calculations
        ↓
Monthly Average
        ↓
Percentage & Rating
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Dashboard
